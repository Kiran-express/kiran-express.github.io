Our vision

One Africa. One connected delivery network.

Moving a parcel across an African city still costs too much and takes too long: transport costs in Africa run 40 to 60% above the global average.Source: Freight News, University of Dar es Salaam study

KIRAN EXPRESS turns every smartphone into a delivery counter and every verified rider into a business. We start in Lagos, then grow market by market.

Every delivery matters

From big cities to villages.

A medicine, a document, a gift: behind every parcel there is a family waiting. We launch in Lagos, with the ambition to deliver wherever our customers live, including far from the big centres.

Our marketplace

One platform. Five players. One fair deal.

  • Customers post a request with their own budget.
  • Drivers bid, the customer chooses.
  • Businesses ship in volume.
  • Affiliates spread the word.
  • A licensed payment partner holds the funds until delivery is confirmed.
How it works

Four steps, from parcel to hand-over.

  1. PostPick-up, drop-off, declared value and your budget.
  2. Get bidsVerified drivers send their offers.
  3. ChooseFunds are held safely until delivery.
  4. DeliveredThe recipient confirms with a 6-digit code.
