Why now

A market speeding up.

  • Africa is the fastest-growing e-commerce region in the world: about +13.9% a year from 2025 to 2030 (ECDB).
  • Nigeria is Africa's largest e-commerce market, with about 26% of revenue (Lengow).
  • African last-mile delivery is set to double, from $1.14B in 2021 to $2.35B by 2030 (Frost & Sullivan).
The problem

Three barriers we remove.

Costly delivery

Transport costs run 40 to 60% above the global average.

Cash risk

Cash payments, theft and untraceable disputes.

No trust

Handing a parcel to a stranger is still risky.

Three revenue lines.

1,700

Why we can win

An edge built in from day one.

  • Asset-light marketplace: no fleet to buy.
  • BID pricing: supply and demand, without subsidies.
  • Guarantors and parcel insurance: trust between strangers.
  • Multi-country from the start: a country is added by configuration.
